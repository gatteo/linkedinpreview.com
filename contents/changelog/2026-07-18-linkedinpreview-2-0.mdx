LinkedIn Preview started as a simple way to format and preview a post. Version 2.0 turns it into a complete LinkedIn content platform - and the core editor stays free, with no signup, forever.

## Your LinkedIn audit and personal plan

The headline feature of 2.0. Connect your profile (via LinkedIn, your profile URL, or just your name) and in about two minutes you get a real audit of your recent posts:

- **Content mix** - how your posts split across personal stories, educational content, opinions, and promotion, with the mix that fits your goal
- **Hooks and CTAs** - how many of your posts open with a scroll-stopping first line and how many invite replies
- **Engagement by category** - which type of content actually earns you reactions
- **Topics** - what you write about today and the adjacent topics worth expanding into

Every number is measured from your real posts - the AI labels, it never invents metrics. The audit then feeds a personalized content plan with pillar post ideas written in your voice.

## Schedule and publish without leaving the app

- **Connect LinkedIn** and publish drafts with one click
- **Post scheduling** with automatic publishing at the time you choose
- **Content calendar** to see your pipeline at a glance
- **Best time to post** suggestions based on your audience

## Carousel creator

Build LinkedIn carousel (PDF) posts in a drag-and-drop slide editor: 14 templates, 11 themes, AI generation from a topic, text, or URL, and watermark-free PDF and PNG export.

## Smarter AI writing

- Generate posts from a topic, your notes, **your voice**, a **file**, or any **URL**
- Chat assistant, quick actions, hook generation, and inline suggestions in the editor
- Everything is **branding-aware**: your positioning, expertise, writing style, and dos and don'ts shape every generation

## Analytics

Import your LinkedIn analytics and get performance insights on what is working - impressions, engagement, and per-post breakdowns, next to the drafts that produced them.

## Accounts and plans

- **Anonymous-first**: the dashboard works without an account, and your data is saved from the first visit
- **Optional account binding** via email or LinkedIn keeps everything across devices - same data, no migration
- **Pro plans** (monthly or lifetime) unlock the full AI toolkit; the editor, preview, and formatting stay free for everyone

As always, the project is [open source](https://github.com/gatteo/linkedinpreview.com) - version 2.0 is tagged and released on GitHub.
