## What Is a LinkedIn Post Mockup Tool?

A LinkedIn post mockup is a visualization tool that creates a realistic preview of how your content will appear in the LinkedIn feed. It is designed to help you catch technical errors, optimize your hook, and ensure your formatting is clean before you hit publish.

## Why You Should Preview Every Post

### 1. Optimize the "See More" Fold

LinkedIn truncates your post after the first few lines, requiring users to click "See more." A mockup tool shows you exactly where your hook gets cut off, allowing you to rewrite it so the truncation happens at a natural, curiosity-inducing pause.

### 2. Perfect Your Unicode Formatting

Since LinkedIn doesn't natively support bold or italics, you rely on Unicode converters. These can occasionally render unexpectedly on certain devices. A preview tool lets you verify that your bolding and list structure is readable across desktop and mobile.

### 3. Check Visual Balance

If you are uploading a carousel or multiple images, a mockup tool helps you check if your media is properly positioned and how it sits within the overall post layout.
